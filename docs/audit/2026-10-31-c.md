## 2. Biased metrics, strawman baselines, Monte Carlo

### 2.1 What the benchmark actually measures
- `coverage_mean_last_orbit` is taken over t in [11351, 17028] s (benchmark.py:126-128 via sim summary), i.e. the steady-state geometry 2.2 h after the failures. Transfer-time loss is invisible (this is why "coverage-hours" was invented, and why it flips the sign, C-8).
- All 15 hungarian rows have coverage 0.26980 (min 0.26979719, max 0.26981); even_split 0.26982 (mean), greedy 0.26980. The metric cannot rank the three recovery policies (they tie to 2e-5); the whole Pareto study is flat for the same reason ("degenerate front" is a property of the metric, not of the allocator).
- Recovery ends with exactly 2 imagers in every pair (leader and downlink must be filled by two of the four survivors), so "coverage retained" is a function of imager count + geometry. do_nothing leaves leader/downlink dead in 9/15 pairs but keeps its imagers, so it scores higher (0.2911 vs 0.2698): the metric rewards not re-electing (D3 known).

### 2.2 Baselines
- `do_nothing`: no re-election, no motion. Critical roles held 6/15 (my script). Strawman for any claim about mission function. (D3, D1.)
- `greedy`: same Step-1 formation sweep as hungarian (allocate.replan, `method="greedy"` toggles Step 2 only) so coverage ties by construction; differs only in Δv (+10% mean, 490.5 vs 444.3 m/s). Not an independent baseline.
- `even_split`: identity assignment, no feasibility sweep, no obstacle check by design (benchmark_policies.py:42-98). Min survivor-dead 722.8 m, Δv 1308 m/s. Documented strawman.
- Missing strong baseline: zero-Δv in-place role swap (map ladder L1). Measured (C-4): mean 0.2570 vs hungarian 0.2698.

### 2.3 Monte Carlo (cubesat/monte_carlo.py)
Confirmed: the sim core consumes no RNG (`grep random|rng|seed cubesat/sim.py allocate.py pd.py hcw.py` finds only the INIT header write at sim.py:218). Across the 20 runs what varies is four scalars (2 failure times +-10%, phase +-30 deg, radial amp x[0.9,1.1]), drawn from one PCG64 stream. Fixed: failure sat ids (1, 2) and modes. Results:
- Seed 42 re-run reproduces GATES.md:46 exactly (hungarian mean 0.269145, worst 0.264992; do_nothing 0.220283 / 0.212978; gain +4.886 / +5.201 / min paired +4.545).
- Seed 43: mean gain +4.872 pts, min paired +4.559 (consistent).
- Seed 7: crashes at run 14 with `RuntimeError: no feasible survivor formation in the sweep grid` (B1) inside the harness's own declared jitter bounds (amp x1.018, phase +28.7 deg, t = 1275.9/1463.8 s). No try/except, so no report is written.
